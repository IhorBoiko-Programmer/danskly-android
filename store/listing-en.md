# Google Play listing — English (en-US)

**Title (≤30):** Danskly: Danish for Ukrainians

**Short description (≤80):** Learn Danish in Ukrainian: words, grammar, speech, Prøve i Dansk 3 prep

**Full description:**

Danskly helps Ukrainian speakers learn Danish, from A2 to B1, and prepare for the Prøve i Dansk 3 exam (module 3.5). The app's interface and explanations are in Ukrainian.

• Daily plan with spaced repetition, rule of the day, exercise and reading
• 1000+ words in 27 everyday topics with all forms and example sentences
• Tap any word or sentence to hear it; slow speech, alphabet and tricky Danish sounds
• 80+ grammar rules with translated examples
• Exercises: fill the gap, build the sentence, write in Danish, verb forms, gender and plural, dictation
• Exam-style reading and listening texts with questions
• Writing tasks (letters, complaints, job applications) with model answers and AI feedback
• AI tutor for conversation practice and explanations
• No ads, no account, progress stays on your phone

Danskly is unofficial and not affiliated with the Danish Ministry of Education or the exam providers.
